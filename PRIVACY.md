# Privacy policy

_Legacy Spell Updater for D&D Beyond. Last updated October 27, 2026._

Legacy Spell Updater does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **What it reads:** the D&D Beyond page you are viewing (monster pages, the encounter builder and
  the combat tracker). It only looks for spell links in monster stat blocks so it can point them
  at the 2024 version.
- **What it stores:** your settings (on/off toggles), saved with Chrome's `storage.sync`. If you're
  signed into Chrome, Chrome syncs them between your own browsers. The extension's author never
  sees them.
- **Network requests:** none. The spell map ships inside the extension.
- **Analytics and tracking:** none.

Questions or concerns: open an issue at https://github.com/stevey/dd55-spells-converter/issues.
